Table Summary

The 6 types of expenditures columns are: Operating, Capital, Transfer payments, Revenues netted against expenditures, Non-budgetary, Total. For each department or agency, totals are displayed for the budgetary and non-budgetary. At the end of the table, ministry totals are displayed for budgetary and non-budgetary authorities.
