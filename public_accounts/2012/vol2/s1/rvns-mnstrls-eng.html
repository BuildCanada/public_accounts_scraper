Table 4 presents revenues for each ministry. The "Total ministerial revenues" column represents revenues from all sources. This column represents tax and other revenues from both external and internal sources. It discloses total ministerial revenues as reported in the "Revenues" statement in each ministerial section of this volume.

Table 4a reconciles total ministerial revenues with external revenues reported in Table 1 of this section and in the Consolidated Statement of Operations and Accumulated Deficit in Section 2 of Volume 1.

Table Summary

The table presents the revenues for each ministry; the table consists of 5 columns: Section, Department or agency; Tax revenues; Other revenues, Total ministerial revenues. Under Other revenues, there are 6 columns: Return on investments; Refunds of previous year's expenditures; Sales of goods and services; Proceeds from the disposal of surplus Crown Assets; Miscellaneous and Total other revenues. The table has 26 Ministries along with its department or agencies, with sub-totals by ministry and at the end of the table, totals for ministerial revenues.
