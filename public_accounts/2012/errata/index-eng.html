Errata

Subsequent to the printing of the Public Accounts of Canada for 2012, corrections were made to the information reported as follows:

  • Volume III, Section 10, International travel expenditures of ministers, parliamentary secretaries, and ministers' staff, page 10.36. Changes have been made to the amount of the Minister for International Trade and Minister for the Asia-Pacific Gateway and to the total of the statement.
