Table Summary

The table presents, in millions of dollars, a 10-year comparative of the consolidated statement of cash flow. It consists of 2 columns: a detailed listing of components; Year ended March 31 divided into 10 columns – 2003; 2004; 2005; 2006; 2007; 2008; 2009; 2010; 2011; 2012. The first series of lines presents the cash provided or used (-) by operating activities. The second series of lines presents the cash used by capital investment activities. The 3 series are totalized under the line called total cash generated or used (-) before financing activities. The fourth series of lines presents the cash used (-) or provided by financing activities followed by a total called net increase or decrease (-) in cash which is the result of the total of the 3 first series of lines plus the total of financing activities. The last 2 lines present the cash and cash equivalents: at beginning of year and at end of year which is the result of cash and cash equivalents at beginning of year plus net increase or decrease (-) in cash.
