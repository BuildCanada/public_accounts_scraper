Revenues, Expenses and Accumulated Deficit

This section provides information on the revenues, expenses and accumulated deficit of the Government as summarized in Table 3.1.

A narrative description is provided for certain accounts reported in some tables. Such description follows the same presentation order as the respective tables.

Table Summary

The table presents, in millions of dollars, a 2-year comparative of the revenues, expenses and accumulated deficit. It consists of 3 columns: a detailed listing of components; 2011-2012; 2010-2011. The first series of lines presents the revenues with a line presenting the total for this section. The second series of lines presents the section for expenses which is divided in sub-sections. All of them contain a sub-total and the final line of the section presents the total of expenses. The annual deficit line is the result of the total revenues minus the total expenses. The last series of lines presents the accumulated deficit and its components.
