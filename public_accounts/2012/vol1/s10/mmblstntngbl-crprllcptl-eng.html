Table 10.2 presents tangible capital assets by main custodian ministries.

Table Summary

The table presents Tangible Capital Assets by main custodian ministries. It consists of 11 columns; the first shows the main custodian ministries for cost, amortization and total. The other columns are Land; Buildings; Works and infrastructure; Machinery and equipment; Vehicles; Leasehold improvements; Assets under construction; Assets under capital leases; Total March 31/2012; Total March 31/2011. The cost portion shows main custodian ministries followed by a subtotal. The next line is for Crown corporations and the last line represents the total for cost. The amortization portion shows main custodian ministries followed by a subtotal. The next line is for Crown corporations and the last line represents the total for amortization. The last portion represents cost minus amortization. There is a subtotal after main custodian ministries in this portion as well.
