Expenditures of ministers' offices

Public Accounts of Canada 2023 Volume III—Top of the page Navigation

  • Previous page: Return on investments
  • Section 10: Table of contents: Section 10: Other government-wide information
  • Next page: Travel expenditures of ministers and parliamentary secretaries

On December 12, 2006, the Federal Accountability Act amended the Access to Information Act to require the head of a department or a ministry of state for the Government of Canada to publish an annual report of all expenses incurred by his or her office and paid out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund. Expenditures presented below are those charged to the Minister's budget (budget totaling $96,462,082) in accordance with the Policies for Ministers' Offices. This table does not include civilian termination benefits and statutory expenditures such as the Minister's salary and motor vehicle allowance, employer contributions to superannuation and certain benefit plans and other items as specified in these policies.

For further guidance on which expenditures are included in the ministers' offices budgets, see Appendix E of these policies.
