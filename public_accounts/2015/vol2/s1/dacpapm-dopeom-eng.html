Table Summary

The table presents details of Other Program Expenses of Other Ministries reported in Table 2a. The table consists of 8 columns: Ministry; Total ministerial net expenditures; Consolidated specified purpose accounts; Accrual and other adjustments; Consolidated Crown corporations and other entities; Tax credits and repayments; internal expenses and Total external expenses. A subtotal exists for all ministries and a total after the "provision for valuation and other items".
