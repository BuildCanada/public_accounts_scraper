Table Summary

The table reconciles total ministerial net expenditures (Table 2) with external expenses reported in Table 1 of this section and in the Consolidated Statement of Operations and Accumulated Deficit in Section 2 of Volume I. The table consists of 8 columns: Description; Total ministerial net expenditures; Consolidated specified purpose accounts; Accrual and other adjustments; Consolidated Crown corporations and other entities; Tax credit and repayments; Internal expenses and Total external expenses. The table displays subtotals by major expense classifications with the ministry and at the end of the table, totals for external expenses.
