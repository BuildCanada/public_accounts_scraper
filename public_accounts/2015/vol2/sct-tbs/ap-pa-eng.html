Table Summary

The table presents the Programs; the table consists of 7 columns: The first column provides the Program description; the next 6 columns give the types of expenditures which are each divided into 2 columns: Total authorities available for use and Authorities used in the current year. The 6 types of expenditures columns are: Operating, Capital, Transfer payments, Revenues netted against expenditures, Non‑budgetary, Total. For each department or agency, totals are displayed for the budgetary and non‑budgetary. At the end of the table, ministry totals are displayed for budgetary and non‑budgetary authorities.
