Erratum No. 1 - December 15, 2015

Please note: this page is wider than normal.

Volume III, Section 8, a change has been made to Payments of Claims Against the Crown, pages 8.15, and 8.16 for the Canada Border Services Agency.

Revised Page - Volume III - Section 8

Table Summary

The table presents, by Ministry, payments of claims against the Crown and consists of two columns. The first column presents particulars and payee of claims against Departments and Agencies by Ministry. Where the situation warrants non‑disclosure, the recipient's name is withheld and identified as such in the table. Claims of less than $1,000 are reported as one amount at the end of each Ministry, together with the total number of such claims. The second column presents the amount of the claim. There is a subtotal row for each Department and Agency and a total row for each Ministry. There is a total at the end of the table.
