Table note 2

Order in Council P.C. 2018—1026, designated the Minister of Democratic Institutions as the appropriate Minister for the Office of the Chief Electoral Officer, effective July 18, 2018. Prior to the transfer, non-statutory expenditures of approximately $9,076,475 were approved by the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada. Previous year's amounts have been restated by $131,485,721.

Return to table note 2 referrer

Table note 3

Order in Council P.C. 2018—1204, designated the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as the appropriate Minister for Public Service Commission, effective September 25, 2018. Prior to the transfer, the Commission was under the responsibility of the Minister of Public Services and Procurement and non-statutory expenditures of approximately $33,341,721 were approved by the Minister of Public Services and Procurement. Previous year's amounts have been restated by $85,668,696.
