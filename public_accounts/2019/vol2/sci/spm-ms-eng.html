Table note 1

Order in Council P.C. 2018–1011 transferred all of the powers, duties and functions under the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council Act, except the powers, duties or function under subsection 4(2) of that Act, from the Minister of Innovation, Science and Economic Development to the Minister of Science, effective July 18, 2018. Prior to the transfer, non-statutory expenditures of approximately $405,039,066 were approved by the Minister of Innovation, Science and Economic Development.
