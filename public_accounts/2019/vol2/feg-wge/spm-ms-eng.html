Table note 3
  • On July 18, 2018, Orders in Council P.C. 2018—1009 and 2018–1010 designated the Minister of Status of Women as the minister responsible to co-ordinate policy with respect to the status of women and administer related programs. Prior to the transfer, the activities of the Office of the Co-ordinator, Status of Women were previously the responsibility of the Minister of Canadian Heritage and Multiculturalism and non-statutory expenditures of approximately $9,855,653 were approved by the Minister of Canadian Heritage and Multiculturalism.
  • On December 13, 2018, pursuant to the Budget Implementation Act, 2018, No. 2, the Minister of Status of Women was deemed appointed Minister for Women and Gender Equality. Pursuant to this act, the Department for Women and Gender Equality Act was enacted, establishing the Department for Women and Gender Equality and dissolving the Office of the Co-ordinator, Status of Women.
