Education costs

Public Accounts of Canada 2016 Volume III - Top of the page Navigation

  • Travel and living costs by commission
  • Return on investments

This statement provides details of education costs incurred in the current year. For the purpose of this statement, an education cost represents the cost of any long-term education activity. A long-term education activity is any training or development activity given to a Government employee and conducted at an educational institution outside of the Public Service that amounted or will amount to a total of 65 working days or more over a maximum period of three (3) years, or that involved or will involve total expenditures in excess of $25,000 (including salary).

Table summary

The table presents details of education costs incurred in the current year and consists of seven columns. The first column presents departments and agencies by ministry. The following columns present the number of employees, salaries, travel and living costs, tuition fees, other expenses and the total for each department and agency. There are totals at the end of the table.
