Table summary

The table presents details of amounts transferred to other ministries to supplement provisions of other votes of the Treasury Board Secretariat and consists of six columns. The first column presents, by ministry, Appropriations by Department and agency. The following columns present, for Amounts transferred from Treasury Board, the vote number, vote 5 Government contingencies, vote 25 operating budget carry forward; vote 30 paylist requirements and vote 33 capital budget carry forward for each appropriation. There are totals at the end of the table.
