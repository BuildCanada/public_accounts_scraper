Section 5—Acquisition of machinery and equipment

Public Accounts of Canada 2016 Volume III - Top of the page Navigation

  • Section 4—Acquisition of land, buildings and works
  • Section 6—Transfer payments

The following statement presents the total amount spent in the current fiscal year for each main category of machinery and equipment by department and agency under each ministry. Amounts reported include both internal and external expenditures. Whereas external expenditures result from transactions between the Government and outside parties, internal expenditures result from transactions involving organizations that are part of the Government of Canada as a reporting entity.

Table summary

The table presents the total amount spent in the current fiscal year for the acquisition of each main category of machinery and equipment by department and agency under each ministry and consists of eleven columns. The first column presents departments and agencies by ministry. The following columns present amounts spent for transportation machinery and equipment, ammunition and weapons, communications equipment, computer related equipment and software, specialized equipment, furniture and fixtures, industrial machinery and equipment, other office equipment (excluding computer/related equipment and software), other machinery and equipment and total for each department and agency. There is a subtotals row for each ministry and a totals row for each column at the end of the table.
