Section 4—Acquisition of land, buildings and works

Public Accounts of Canada 2016 Volume III - Top of the page Navigation

  • Section 3—Professional and special services
  • Section 5—Acquisition of machinery and equipment

The following statement presents the total amount spent in the current fiscal year by department and agency under each ministry. Amounts reported include both internal and external expenditures. Whereas external expenditures result from transactions between the Government and outside parties, internal expenditures result from transactions involving organizations that are part of the Government of Canada as a reporting entity.

The detailed information is available at Acquisition of land, buildings and works supplementary information. This information includes for each Government program:

  • the total amount spent in the current fiscal year;
  • details for contracts of $250,000 or over ($25,000 or over for cost plus contracts). Such details include the name and location of the contractor, a brief description and location of the project, the amount contracted, the current year's expenditures (i.e. cash payments and accrued charges) and the total expenditures to date; and
  • the total amount of the current year's expenditures for contracts under $250,000 (cost plus contracts of under $25,000) with the total number of contractors.
Table summary

The table presents the total amount spent in the current fiscal year on the acquisition of land, buildings and works by department and agency under each ministry and consists of six columns. The first column presents departments and agencies by ministry. The following columns present amounts spent for acquisition of land, acquisition of engineering works, acquisition of non-residential buildings, acquisition of residential buildings and total. There is a subtotals row for each ministry and a totals row for each column at the end of the table.
