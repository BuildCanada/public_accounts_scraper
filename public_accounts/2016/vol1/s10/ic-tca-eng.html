Table 10.2 presents tangible capital assets by main custodian ministries.

Table summary

The table presents, in thousands of dollars, the tangible capital assets by main custodian ministries. It consists of eleven columns: the listing of main custodian ministries; Land; Buildings; Works and infrastructure; Machinery and equipment; Vehicles; Leasehold improvements; Assets under construction; Assets under capital leases; Total at March 31 of the current year; Total at March 31 of the previous year. The first series of rows presents the capital assets at cost by ministry with a subtotal, followed by the consolidated Crown corporations and other entities, and the gross total for the series. The second series of rows presents the accumulated amortization by ministry with a subtotal, followed by the consolidated Crown corporations and other entities, and the total for the series. The third series of rows presents the total net capital assets by ministry—result obtained by subtracting the accumulated amortization from the capital assets at cost, a subtotal and the consolidated Crown corporations and other entities. A final row presents the total net capital assets.
