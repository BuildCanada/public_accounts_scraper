The 6 types of expenditures columns are: operating, capital, transfer payments, revenues netted against expenditures, budgetary total, non-budgetary. For each department or agency, totals are displayed for the budgetary and non-budgetary. At the end of the table, ministry totals are displayed for budgetary and non-budgetary authorities.
