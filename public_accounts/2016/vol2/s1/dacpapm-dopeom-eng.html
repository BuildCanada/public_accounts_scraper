Table summary

The table presents details of other program expenses of other ministries reported in table 2a. The table consists of 8 columns: ministry; total ministerial net expenditures; consolidated specified purpose accounts; accrual and other adjustments; consolidated Crown corporations and other entities; tax credits and repayments; internal expenses and total external expenses. A sub-total exists for all ministries and a total after the "provision for valuation and other items".
