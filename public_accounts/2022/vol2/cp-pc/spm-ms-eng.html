Table note 2

Order in Council P.C. 2021—0934, designated the Minister of Intergovernmental Affairs, Infrastructure and Communities as the appropriate Minister for the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat, effective October 26, 2021. Prior to the transfer, the Secretariat was under the responsibility of the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada and non-statutory expenditures of approximately $2,416,176 were approved by the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada. Previous year's amounts have been restated by $4,285,564.

Table note 3

Prior to the transfer, the Commission was under the responsibility of the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada and non-statutory expenditures of approximately $285,627 were approved by the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada. Previous year's amounts have been restated by $315,520.
