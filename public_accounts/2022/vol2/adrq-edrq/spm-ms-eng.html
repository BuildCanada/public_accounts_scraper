Table note 2

Pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act, the Minister of Sport and Minister responsible for the Economic Development Agency of Canada for the Regions of Quebec has been designated as the appropriate Minister for the Economic Development Agency of Canada for the Regions of Quebec, effective October 26, 2021. Prior to the transfer, non-statutory expenditures of approximately $151,298,008 were approved by the Minister of Innovation, Science and Industry.
