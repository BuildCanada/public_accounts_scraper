Table 3a reconciles total ministerial net expenditures (Table 3) with total external expenses reported in Table 1 of this section and in the Consolidated Statement of Operations and Accumulated Deficit in Section 2 of Volume I. The reconciling items include the expenditures of the consolidated specified purpose accounts, the accrual and other adjustments, the expenses of the consolidated Crown corporations and other entities, the tax credits and repayments, and the elimination of internal expenses and internal revenues netted against expenses.

Table Summary

The table reconciles total ministerial net expenditures (Table 3) with total external expenses reported in Table 1 of this section and in the Consolidated Statement of Operations and Accumulated Deficit in Section 2 of Volume I. The table consists of 8 columns: Description; Total ministerial net expenditures; Consolidated specified purpose accounts, Accrual and other adjustments; Consolidated Crown corporations and other entities; Tax credits and repayments; Less; Total External Expenses. Under Less, there are 2 columns: Internal expenses; Internal revenues netted against expenses. There is a sub-total line displayed for Total Gross expenditures and at the end of the table, totals are displayed after other reconciliation items.
