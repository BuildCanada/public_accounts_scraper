Erratum No. 6 - November 26, 2013

Please note: this page is wider than normal.

Volume I, Section 1, Financial Statements Discussion and Analysis, Table 1.3, Government of Canada – Detailed Consolidated Statement of Change in Net Debt. A footnote has been added to explain the increase of the 2012 opening balance.

Revised Page

Table Summary

The table presents, in millions of dollars, a ten-year comparative of the consolidated statement of change in net debt. It consists of two columns: a detailed listing of components; Year ended March 31 divided into ten columns representing, in chronological order, the previous nine years and the current year. The first row is the net debt at beginning of year followed by the transition adjustment. Then, a series of rows presents the change in net debt during the year. The following row presents the other comprehensive (income) or loss. Then, a subtotal called net (decrease) or increase in net debt follows just before the last row of this table which is the net debt at end of year.
