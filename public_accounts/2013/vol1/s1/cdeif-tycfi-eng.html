Table Summary

The table presents, in millions of dollars, a ten-year comparative of the consolidated statement of operations and accumulated deficit. It consists of two columns: a detailed listing of components; Year ended March 31 divided into ten columns representing, in chronological order, the previous nine years and the current year. The first series of rows presents the revenues which are divided in sub-sections. All of them contain a sub-total and the final row of the section presents the total of revenues. The second series of rows presents the expenses which are divided in sub-sections. All of them contain a sub-total and the final row of the section presents the total of expenses. The annual (surplus) or deficit row is the result of the total revenues minus the total expenses. The last series of rows presents the accumulated deficit and its components.

Table Summary

The table presents, in millions of dollars, a ten-year comparative of the consolidated statement of cash flow. It consists of two columns: a detailed listing of components; Year ended March 31 divided into ten columns representing, in chronological order, the previous nine years and the current year. The first series of rows presents the cash provided or (used) by operating activities. The second series of rows presents the cash used by capital investment activities. The third series of rows presents the cash provided or (used) by investing activities. The three series are totalized under the row called total cash generated or (used) before financing activities. The fourth series of rows presents the cash (used) or provided by financing activities followed by a total called net increase or (decrease) in cash which is the result of the total of the three first series of rows plus the total of financing activities. The last two rows present the cash and cash equivalents: at beginning of year and at end of year which is the result of cash and cash equivalents at beginning of year plus net increase or (decrease) in cash.
