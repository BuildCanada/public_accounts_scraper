Table 10.2 presents tangible capital assets by main custodian ministries.

Table Summary

The table presents, in thousands of dollars, the tangible capital assets by main custodian ministries. It consists of eleven columns: the listing of main custodian ministries; land; buildings; works and infrastructure; machinery and equipment; vehicles; leasehold improvements; assets under construction; assets under capital leases; total at March 31 of the current year; Total at March 31 of the previous year. The first series of lines presents the capital assets at cost by main ministries followed by a total then by the consolidated Crown corporations. The last line presents the gross total capital assets. The second series of lines presents the accumulated amortization by main ministries followed by a total then by the consolidated Crown corporations. The last line presents the total accumulated amortization. The third series of lines presents the total net capital assets by main ministries which is the capital assets at cost minus the accumulated amortization followed by a total then by the consolidated Crown corporations. The last line presents the total net capital assets.
