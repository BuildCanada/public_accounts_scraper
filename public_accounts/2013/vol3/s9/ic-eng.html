The table presents for Industry, the expenditure by province and territory, for each federal-provincial shared-cost programs, the current year and previous year expenditures and the expenditures from the inception of the program. The table consists of sixteen columns. The first column presents, by Department and Agency, each federal-provincial shared-cost program name, the current year, the previous year and there is a line for expenditures from inception. The following columns present expenditure amounts by province and territory as follows: Newfoundland and Labrador, Prince Edward Island, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta, British Columbia, Total Provinces, Northwest Territories, Nunavut, Yukon Territory and Total.
