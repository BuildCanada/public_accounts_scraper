Return on investments

Public Accounts of Canada 2013 Volume III - Top of the page Navigation

Education costs Table of Contents - Section 10 Expenditures of Ministers' Offices
(this page is wider than normal)

This statement provides details for the current year of the category of other revenues called return on investments. Return on investments consists mainly of interest from loans and advances, dividends from investments, and transfer of profits, as recorded by departments before any adjustments are made. For the purposes of presentation in the financial statements, amounts shown here for foreign exchange accounts and enterprise Crown corporations have been respectively reclassified to net foreign exchange revenues and Crown corporation revenues in Sections 2 and 3 of Volume I and Table 4a of Volume II.

Table Summary

The table presents details for the current year of the category of other revenues called return on investments and consists of two columns. The first column presents, by account, the main elements of returns on investments, by Department and Agency. The second column presents Amounts realized in the current year for each element. There is a sub-total line for each Return on investments account. Following the sub-total of the last account reported the information is summarized by Interest, Transfer of profits, Dividends and Other. There is a total at the end of the table.
