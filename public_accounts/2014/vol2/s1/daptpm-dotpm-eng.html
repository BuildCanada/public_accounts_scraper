Table Summary

The table presents details by ministry of other transfer payments reported in Table 2a. The table consists of 8 columns: Ministry; Total ministerial net expenditures; Consolidated specified purpose accounts; Accrual and other adjustments, Consolidated Crown corporations and other entities; Tax credits and repayments; Internal expenses and Total external expenses. A subtotal exists for all ministries and a total after the "provision for valuation and other items".
