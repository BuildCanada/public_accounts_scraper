Erratum No. 1 - November 26, 2014

Please note: this page is wider than normal.

Volume III, Section 3, Professional and Special Services, pages 3.7 and 3.13. Changes were made to amounts in the "Other services" for the department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development and in Section 8, Payments of Claims Against the Crown, pages 8.7 and 8.25. A change has been made to the amount for the department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development.

Unpublished detailed information relating to Section 3, Volume III, Professional and Special Services, pages 40 and 45. A change has been made to the amount spent by payee for the department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development. The revised information is highlighted.
