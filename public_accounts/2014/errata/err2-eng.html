Erratum No. 2 - December 4, 2014

Please note: this page is wider than normal.

Volume III, Section 3, Professional and Special Services, pages 3.5 and 3.13. Changes were made to amounts in the "Other services" for the department of Employment and Social Development and in Section 8, Payments of Claims Against the Crown, pages 8.3, 8.4 and 8.25. A change has been made to the amount for the department Employment and Social Development.

Unpublished detailed information relating to Section 3, Volume III, Professional and Special Services, pages 20 and 23. A change has been made to the amount spent by payee for the department of Employment and Social Development. The revised information is highlighted.
