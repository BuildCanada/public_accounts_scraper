Payments of claims against the Crown

Public Accounts of Canada 2014 Volume III - Top of the page Navigation

  • Table of Contents - Section 8
  • Ex gratia payments

This statement provides, by ministry and by departments and agencies, details for all payments of claims against the Crown of $1,000 or over. Claims of less than $1,000 are reported as one amount at the end of each department and agency, together with the total number of such claims.Where the situation warrants non-disclosure, the recipient's name may be withheld from publication.

Table Summary

The table presents, by Ministry, payments of claims against the Crown and consists of two columns. The first column presents particulars and payee of claims against Departments and Agencies by Ministry. Where the situation warrants non-disclosure, the recipient's name is withheld and identified as such in the table. Claims of less than $1,000 are reported as one amount at the end of each Ministry, together with the total number of such claims. The second column presents the amount of the claim. There is a sub-total line for each Department and Agency and a total line for each Ministry. There is a total at the end of the table.
